# CALL SHEET — keep this open, nothing else

**Goal: understand her business + book call two. That's all.**
She talks 80%. You talk 20%.

---

## Before you dial (2 minutes)

- Quiet room, headphones, phone charged
- Notepad open — **type her exact words**, not summaries
- Deck open in another tab **as backup only** — see bottom
- If she says "let's talk now" → say yes. Don't reschedule twice.

---

## Open

> "Thanks for making time. Before I show you anything, I want to understand the business properly. Can I ask you some questions? I'll be taking notes."

---

## The questions — in this order

**1. Warm open — use her own Story**
> "I saw your story, 'building something crazy for all my girlies.' Tell me about that."

**2. The vision**
> "Tell me about neXtHer. How did you start thinking about it?"
> "If it goes well, where do you see it in 2 years?"

*Then shut up. Let her run.*

**3. What exists**
> "What all is ready right now? Website, any course, anything?"
> "Has anyone paid you for neXtHer related work till now?"
> "Your bio says trusted by 100+ women — paid clients or free sessions?"

**4. Who it's for**
> "Think of one woman who is your ideal client. Describe her. What is she stuck on?"

**5. The product**
> "What will she buy from you, and what price are you thinking?"
> "When you say AI, what exactly will you teach her? Walk me through it."
> "What do you use AI for in your own work daily?"

**6. Her own words — the easy win**
> "On Threads you described yourself as a coach who helps women get hotter, richer, more energized and more loved. Honestly I thought that was sharper than your Instagram bio. Is that how you think about it?"

**7. The book club — asked lightly, not as a gotcha**
> "I saw you were starting a book club also. How does that fit with everything else?"

**8. THE BIG ONE — ask softly, curious, not challenging**
> "One thing I want to understand. Most of your posts are about relationships and marriage. But what you told me is about AI and income. How are these two connected for you?"

*Then say nothing. Count to ten in your head. Let her think.*

**9. What she wants from you**
> "When you say production partner, what exactly do you picture me doing?"
> "Which part do you want to take off your plate first?"
> "How much time can you give this every week?"

**10. Practical**
> "What timeline do you have in mind?"
> "Have you thought about a budget for this?"

---

## Close — don't skip this

> "This was really helpful. Give me a few days, I'll put together how I'd approach it based on what you told me. Can we talk again on **[day]**?"

**Book call two before you hang up. That is the only outcome that matters today.**

## 3 traps

| She says | You say |
| --- | --- |
| "How much?" | "Let me understand properly first, then I'll come back with real numbers. I don't want to quote you for the wrong thing." |
| "How many followers can you get me?" | "Anyone promising a number is guessing. Katie took a year and 369 posts. You've posted 34." |
| "Should I stop relationship content?" | "I have a view, I want to discuss it properly next time. Can I come back on it?" |

**Stuck on anything else:**
> "Good question. Let me check properly and tell you correctly, rather than saying something wrong now."

---

## About the deck

**Don't present it.** You're still learning the business — presenting now means guessing out loud.

**If she pushes to see something:** show slide 4 only — Katie, 415K, the market gap. It's exciting and flattering.

**Do NOT show:**
- Slide 3 (four businesses) or 7 (never spoken on camera) — criticism before she's felt heard
- Slide 10 (pricing) — never before you've scoped it

> "I've got a deck, but honestly it's built on assumptions until I hear from you. Let me update it properly and walk you through it next time."

---

## Remember

She's a solo founder with 2 kids starting from scratch. She's probably nervous too.
**You're not being tested. You're the one asking the questions.**

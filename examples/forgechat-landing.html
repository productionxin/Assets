Running in four steps

  1. Get a serverAny VPS with 2 GB or more of RAM, ports 80 and 443 open, and a domain pointed at it.
  2. Start the containersDeployment is Docker-based. For a local trial, Docker Desktop on Mac or Windows is enough.
  3. Connect WhatsAppLink your Meta WhatsApp Cloud API number to the inbox.
  4. Invite your teamEveryone answers from the same inbox, and the history stays in your PostgreSQL database.

Questions worth asking first

Is it actually free?

Yes. ForgeChat is fair-code, distributed under the Sustainable Use License. You can use it for personal or business purposes and share it non-commercially at no cost. What the licence does not permit is reselling it or offering it as a paid hosting service.

Where does my customer data live?

In your PostgreSQL database, on the server you deployed to. That is the point of self-hosting — no third party holds the conversation history.

Do I need to be a developer?

No coding is required for basic setup, and the automation builder is drag-and-drop. You do need to be comfortable running Docker on a VPS and pointing a domain at it.

What does it cost to run?

Only your own infrastructure — a VPS with 2 GB of RAM and a domain. There are no per-seat fees, and WhatsApp messaging charges are billed by Meta directly.

Can I use my own AI provider?

Yes. The AI agents connect to OpenAI or Claude with your own API keys, including for voice-note transcription.

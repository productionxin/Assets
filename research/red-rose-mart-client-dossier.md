# Red Rose Mart — Client Research Dossier

**Prepared:** 30 July 2026
**Subject:** Red Rose Mart / RED ROSE SUPERMARKET (Red Rose Group of Companies, Hyderabad)
**Purpose:** Pre-engagement research ahead of standing up a marketing team for this client.

> **Research constraint — read this first.** This environment's network policy blocked
> all direct page fetches (gateway 403 on CONNECT), including `redrosemart.com`
> itself and every news domain. Everything below comes from web-search result
> summaries plus the client-supplied Instagram and LinkedIn screenshots. Figures
> quoted from trade press are **claimed, not verified** — flagged inline. A first
> engagement task should be to re-verify the numbers directly with the client and
> to crawl the site from an unrestricted network.

## 1. Who the client actually is

Red Rose Mart is the modern-retail arm of the **Red Rose Group of Companies**, a
Hyderabad family conglomerate. This matters for positioning: the client is not a
grocery startup, it is a 45-year-old industrial group entering retail with
balance-sheet backing.

- Group origin: **Red Rose Tobacco**, a small tobacco manufacturing and sales unit
  founded in **1980** in Hyderabad. Trade press also describes a "35-year legacy
  that began with a tyre puncture shop" — the two origin stories conflict and
  should be reconciled with the client before any brand-story work.
- Group divisions today (six): FMCG, Pan Masala, Real Estate, Hospitality,
  LED & Electrical, Health Equipment. ([redrosegroup.in](https://www.redrosegroup.in/About))
- Adjacent group assets that are marketing-relevant: **Red Rose Palace**, a
  Nampally wedding/events venue, and Red Rose Home Industries (manufacturing).
  There is an existing event and hospitality capability to leverage.
- Separate social identity for the parent: [@redrosegrouphyd](https://www.instagram.com/redrosegrouphyd/) on Instagram.

### Leadership

| Name | Role | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Syed Zeeshanuddin | Managing Director, retail division | Co-originator of the Red Rose Mart concept |
| Zia Ur Rahman | CEO (also "Zia Rahman") | Reported as **25 years old**, youngest board executive; profiled as a young-entrepreneur story; appears as an employee on the LinkedIn company page |
| Sreedhar Tejomurtula | Business Head, retail | Operational lead on the expansion |

The 25-year-old-CEO angle is already being used in press ([RNI Business profile](https://business.republicnewsindia.com/zia-ur-rahman-young-entrepreneur-from-hyderabad-whos-transforming-indian-retail/)) and is a live founder-brand asset — see §7.

## 2. Business scale and trajectory (claimed figures)

| Metric | Reported value | Source confidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Store count | **5 stores** across Hyderabad and beyond | Trade press, late 2025 |
| Retail footprint | ~**80,000 sq ft**, plus a **75,000 sq ft flagship** about to open | Trade press — flagship nearly doubles the estate |
| Revenue Y1 → Y2 | **₹75 cr → ₹125 cr** (~67% growth) | Trade press, unaudited |
| Transactions | **~1.5 lakh bill cuts / month** | Trade press |
| Sales density | **~₹1,950 per sq ft per day** | Trade press |
| Stated ambition | **100+ stores over the next decade** | Founders, at launch |

Timeline: first store opened at **Falaknuma** (~26,000 sq ft, on the
Falaknuma–Charminar arterial road) in early 2023 to genuine crowd scenes — press
reported shoppers thronging before the doors opened. Second store followed in
**December 2023**. Four outlets within two years, five by late 2025.

Known/likely locations from listings and the client's own Instagram bio:
**Falaknuma / Madina Colony**, **Kanchan Bagh**, **Moin Bagh (Moueen Bagh,
DRDL X Road)**, **Shadnagar**, **Tolichowki**, **Saidabad**, **Baber Bagh**, plus a
**Vattepally warehouse** and a Chandrayanagutta "Red Rose Food & Beverages" entity.
Store list needs confirming — third-party directories are stale and duplicated.

**Read:** this is a fast-scaling regional chain at the inflection point where
word-of-mouth stops being enough. Five stores is where marketing shifts from
"open and they will come" to needing per-store demand generation, and the
75,000 sq ft flagship is a once-only launch moment.

## 3. Positioning as it stands

- Tagline in market: **"Apne shahar mein sabse sasta"** (cheapest in your own city) —
  used verbatim in the Instagram bio.
- LinkedIn strapline, in English: **"Where Quality Meets Affordability."**
- Core promise: **everyday low prices, minimum 6% discount across products,
  365 days a year** — an EDLP model, explicitly DMart-like rather than
  promotion-cycle-driven.
- Format: full-service supermarket, not a discount box. The Falaknuma store carries
  FMCG, a coffee shop, meat, fruit & vegetables, a sweet-meat counter, cosmetics,
  imitation jewellery, a pharmacy, and a **live bakery operated by Pista House** —
  a genuinely strong local co-brand.
- Private label: a **Red Rose Basmati** line exists (retail and 30 kg bulk packs,
  third-party listings suggest manufacture via Sona Cereal Products), and "deep
  private labels" are cited as a margin driver. The Instagram grid shows **"RR
  Basmati & masala"** and an apparel/tote line branded **"RR STYLE"**.

**Tension to resolve early:** the price message ("sabse sasta") and the quality
message ("Quality Meets Affordability") are pulling in different directions, and
the two are running on different platforms in different languages. An EDLP brand
can hold both, but only with an explicit hierarchy. This is the single highest-value
strategic deliverable at kickoff.

## 4. Digital and marketing presence — audit

### Instagram — [@redrosemart](https://www.instagram.com/redrosemart/)
The strongest owned asset by a wide margin.

- **29.7K followers**, 27 following, active grid.
- Bio does real work: home-delivery number **9030303752**, all branch names,
  timings **8:00 AM – 11:00 PM**, tagline, website link.
- Content mix is more ambitious than typical regional grocery: a **Red Bull /
  Oracle Red Bull Racing F1 activation** (an F1 car in-store, "Who parked F1 car at
  Red Rose Mart?", "RedRose × Red Bull. One delivery. Unlimited attention"), title
  sponsorship of **"Daawat Dastarkhan"** (6 September, Hyderabad), Ramzan
  (Marhaba Ramzan), Sankranti, Dussehra and cricket highlight reels, warehouse
  and store-exterior films, and a monthly offers cadence ("AUGUST OFFERS — Coming
  Soon").
- **Note:** search could not independently corroborate either the Red Bull
  activation or the Daawat Dastarkhan title sponsorship. Both appear only on the
  client's own grid. Confirm scope, rights, and whether the Red Bull tie is a
  distribution relationship (Red Bull as a stocked brand) rather than an F1
  sponsorship — the creative implies the former, dressed as the latter.

**Verdict:** the client already has creative ambition and a real audience. They do
not need to be convinced that marketing matters. What is missing is measurement,
consistency and conversion — see §7.

### LinkedIn — RED ROSE SUPERMARKET
Badly under-built, and inconsistent with reality.

- **80 followers** vs 29.7K on Instagram. 11 employees listed.
- Company size shown as **201–500 employees**, type **Partnership**, industry
  Retail Groceries, HQ Hyderabad. The 201–500 headcount against 11 listed
  employees means almost no employee-profile linkage — a recruitment and
  credibility gap for a chain hiring at 100-store pace.
- LinkedIn's own "similar pages" module is surfacing **National Mart — India Ka
  HyperMart (Secunderabad)** as a peer, and otherwise unrelated "Red Rose"
  entities in London and Dubai. There is a **brand-name-collision problem**: Red
  Rose Properties, Red Rose Restaurant Panjagutta, Red Rose flower wholesalers,
  Red Rose Tobacco and a UK non-profit all compete for the same search term. This
  materially hurts discoverability and is a naming/SEO problem worth costing.

### Website — redrosemart.com
**Could not be assessed.** Blocked by the network policy here. Search indexes it as
"Redrosemart.com – redrosemart.com" — a page title that is a literal domain
echo, which is a strong signal of an unconfigured template with no SEO
foundation. Treat a full site audit as an unblocked Day-1 task.

### Other surfaces
- **Facebook:** [RedrosemartInd](https://www.facebook.com/redrosemartind/) and a separate
  [Red Rose Group of Companies](https://www.facebook.com/redrosegroupofcompanies/) page — split presence.
- **Directories:** listings on Justdial (Falaknuma **1,905 ratings**; Saidabad

## 5. Reputation — what customers actually say

Aggregate sentiment is good but slipping in the ways scale-up retail usually slips.
Reported **~4.2 stars on Google across 1,660+ reviews**.

**Working:** neat layout, fresh produce, affordable prices, frequent discounts,
range breadth, friendly staff, welcoming experience.

**Recurring complaints — treat as a marketing risk register:**
- **Long checkout queues** — the most frequent single complaint. A price-led,
  high-footfall EDLP model with slow tills erodes the value promise directly.
- Staff described as unprofessional or dismissive in some reviews.
- **"Deceptive calls about gift hampers"** — this is the serious one. Either a
  scam impersonating the brand or an outbound tactic that reads as one. Needs
  clarification immediately; it is a trust and possibly a legal exposure, and it
  interacts badly with the lucky-draw mechanic below.
- Unclear pricing.
- Fresh produce and imported lines sometimes near expiry.
- Missing familiar brands; charging for carry bags.
- Thinner fresh selection than rival supermarkets.

**Existing promo mechanic:** daily lucky draws for bills above ₹1,000, plus a bumper
draw with a **Toyota Glanza**, TV, washing machine and accessories as prizes. Loud,
effective for footfall, and completely undata-capturing as currently run — the same
spend routed through a registered loyalty programme would build a first-party
customer list.

## 6. Competitive context

Red Rose is a five-store chain in one of India's most contested grocery markets.

| Competitor | Scale | Threat type |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Vijetha Supermarkets** | 100+ stores across Telangana/AP | Direct; described as Hyderabad's largest food & grocery player |
| **Ratnadeep Retail** | 180+ stores across Telangana, AP, Karnataka | Direct; premium-leaning, strong brand equity |
| **DMart (Avenue Supermarts)** | ₹60,000 cr+ revenue | Direct on EDLP — owns the "cheapest" claim nationally |
| **Reliance Retail** (Smart / Smart Bazaar / JioMart) | ₹3.3 lakh cr+ | Omnichannel, unmatched depth |
| **More Retail, Heritage Fresh, National Mart** | Regional | Direct, expanding into the same emerging catchments |
| **Blinkit / Zepto / Swiggy Instamart** | q-commerce GMV ~₹11,000 cr in Jan 2026 alone, roughly doubling YoY; Blinkit ~45–50% share | **Structural.** Eats top-up and impulse baskets, the highest-margin trips |

Two things follow. First, **"sabse sasta" is a claim DMart already owns at national
scale** — Red Rose cannot win a pure price war and needs a defensible second axis
(old-city proximity, Hyderabadi cultural fluency, halal/local range, fresh, service,
the Pista House bakery). Second, **q-commerce is the real threat, not the other
supermarkets.** Phone-based home delivery is not a competitive answer to 10-minute
delivery. Regional chains that survive this either build genuine digital order
capture or lean hard into the destination-trip and fresh advantages q-commerce
cannot replicate.

Red Rose's genuine, non-obvious advantage: **deep credibility in Hyderabad's old
city** — Falaknuma, Charminar, Madina Colony, Chandrayanagutta, Tolichowki. This is
a large, underserved, culturally specific catchment that national chains serve
poorly and market to generically. That is the moat worth building the brand on.

## 7. Where a marketing team should start

Ordered by value, not by ease.

**Immediate (weeks 1–4)**
1. **Verify the numbers and the store list** with the client — revenue, store
   count, footfall, the two conflicting origin stories, and the true nature of the
   Red Bull and Daawat Dastarkhan relationships.
2. **Resolve the "gift hamper calls" issue.** Establish whether it is brand
   impersonation or an in-house tactic, then either publish a fraud warning or
   kill the tactic. Unaddressed, it undermines every trust message that follows.
3. **Website audit and rebuild scope.** The domain-echo page title suggests
   there is effectively nothing there. Minimum viable: store locator with hours,
   offers page, delivery request capture, careers page.
4. **Local SEO / Google Business Profile cleanup** across all five stores plus
   the warehouse. Claim, deduplicate, correct hours, respond to reviews. Cheapest
   available win given 1,660+ reviews already sitting unmanaged.

**Near term (months 2–3)**
5. **Positioning workstream** — settle the price-vs-quality hierarchy, and find the
   second axis beyond price. Reconcile the Urdu tagline and the English strapline
   into one architecture. Address the "Red Rose" name-collision problem.
6. **Convert the lucky draw into a loyalty programme.** Same budget, same
   excitement, but with registration — turning 1.5 lakh monthly transactions into a
   first-party customer list. This is the highest-ROI single change available.
7. **Flagship launch campaign** for the 75,000 sq ft store. A 75k sq ft opening in
   Hyderabad is a PR event, not a poster run; the Falaknuma crowd scenes prove
   the playbook works and can be scaled.
8. **Fix the checkout complaint in-store**, then market the fix. Ops problem,
   marketing consequence.

**Structural (months 3–6)**
9. **Digital order capture** — WhatsApp commerce as the pragmatic first step given
   the existing phone-order habit, then evaluate an app. Do not attempt to
   out-deliver Blinkit; own the planned weekly basket instead.
10. **LinkedIn and employer brand.** 80 followers for a 200–500-person business
    hiring toward 100 stores is a recruitment cost. Employee-profile linkage,
    leadership posting, careers content.
11. **Founder brand for Zia Ur Rahman.** A 25-year-old CEO scaling a family
    tobacco business into modern retail is a story national business media will
    take. Press is already circling; make it deliberate.
12. **Private label brand system.** "RR Basmati", "RR STYLE" and Red Rose Basmati
    are currently three unrelated marks. Private label is the stated margin engine
    and deserves a coherent architecture.

**Team shape implied:** a brand/content lead who can work in Urdu, Hindi and
English for the old-city audience; a performance and local-SEO owner; a social/video
producer (the Instagram bar is already set high); and — critically — a CRM/loyalty
owner, because right now the client has 1.5 lakh monthly transactions and no
customer data.

## 8. Open questions for the client

- Actual current store count, locations, and the flagship's opening date.
- Are the ₹125 cr / 1.5 lakh bill-cut / ₹1,950 per sq ft figures audited?
- What is the Red Bull relationship contractually? And Daawat Dastarkhan?
- What is happening with the gift-hamper calls?
- Is there any customer database today, in any form? Any POS-linked mobile capture?
- What is the current marketing spend, and who executes it now (in-house, agency, founder-led)?
- Is Pista House an exclusive partnership, and is it replicated at every store?
- Is Red Rose Basmati owned brand or licensed? Who controls the mark?
- Which group entity holds the retail business, and does group brand equity
  (tobacco, pan masala) help or hurt the grocery brand with the target audience?
